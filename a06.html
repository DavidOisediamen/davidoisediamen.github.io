Generative AI Ethics Risk Memo

A reusable memo for the cases where the law is silent: an ethical analysis method, stakeholder harm mapping, a disclosure standard and four worked memos.

v1.0Generative AIEthics
Full document, attached to the original post
Why it exists

This is lawful. Should we do it anyway?

Most governance instruments ask whether a use case is lawful. This one asks the next question, which is where generative AI creates most of its reputational exposure.

Imitating a contractor’s style, cloning a voice, transcribing a meeting without asking: none is reliably unlawful, and all of them damage trust. The memo gives a structured way to reason about them and to record the reasoning.

The rules it enforces

Three rules that make it a control, not a form.

1
Three lenses, not oneLegal, ethical and prudential. A use case that clears legal review and still produces unease has been assessed on one lens out of three.
2
A preference is not an ethical choiceA preference affects only the person making it; an ethical choice affects others. The first question is which one you are looking at.
3
Do not claim the concerns awayUsing generative AI while stating its unresolved problems openly is defensible. Using it while claiming they do not exist is not.
