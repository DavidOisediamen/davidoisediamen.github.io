Artefact 8 · Stage 3 · Assessment and risk

AI Data Governance Assessment

A six-domain assessment of the data beneath an AI system: source, quality, bias, privacy, security and monitoring, with vendor change control and a data readiness gate.

v1.0NIST AI RMFDAMA-DMBOKPriority artefact
Full document in my LinkedIn Featured section
Why it exists

A model is a function of its data.

The properties people attribute to models, such as bias, unreliability, opacity and drift, are almost always properties of the data that produced them.

That makes sequencing the point. Fairness cannot be fixed at the interface and provenance cannot be reconstructed once the pipeline has run, so the assessment is completed before the model is built.

The rules it enforces

Three rules that make it a control, not a form.

1
Defects in data become structuralBias in training data becomes a property of the model weights; the only remedies are retraining or not deploying.
2
A procured model is a data source that changes without askingContract for notice of material model changes, and log a version identifier on every output.
3
Nothing proceeds while a condition reads Not readyThe data readiness gate is the determination, not a formality.
What's inside

Six domains, then the readiness gate.
