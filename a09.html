Artefact 9 · Stage 4 · Operate and prove

AI Product Governance Lifecycle

Eleven stages from idea to retirement, with governance at every transition: roles, gates, evidence, explainability techniques and operations controls.

v1.0EU AI ActProduct governance
Request the full document
Why it exists

At what stage do I involve governance, and what does it need from me?

Every other artefact sits at the governance layer. This one sits at the product layer and answers the question teams building AI most often cannot.

Each of the eleven stages has a defined transition, a governance gate, required evidence and the controls that apply. The lifecycle is embedded inside the software development lifecycle, not run alongside it.

The rules it enforces

Three rules that make it a control, not a form.

1
Translation, not relayA requirement passed from legal to the developers without being understood by either side loses fidelity at each handoff. That gap is where most AI product compliance failures start.
2
Oversight is designed at Stage 6, not added at Stage 9Reviewer role, authority, information, time per case and the automation-bias control are specified at architecture.
3
The explainability trade-off is decided deliberatelyMade by the accountable person at Stage 6 and reviewed at Stage 8. A trade-off made silently is a finding.
