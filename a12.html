Artefact 12 · Stage 2 · Intake and classification

AI Governance Maturity Assessment

A scored diagnostic across five dimensions and four tiers, with evidence required at every level, a gap analysis method and a prioritised roadmap.

v1.0NIST AI RMF
Full document, attached to the original post
Why it exists

Self-assessed maturity overstates position by one to two tiers.

The first step in building a programme is an honest view of where the organisation stands. This instrument produces it.

It differs from most maturity models in one respect: every score must be evidenced. A dimension cannot be claimed at a tier without producing the artefacts listed for that tier.

The rules it enforces

Three rules that make it a control, not a form.

1
The evidence ruleA criterion scores at a tier only if its evidence can be produced on request, dated and owned.
2
Absent is not PartialA zero means nothing is happening. Averaging it into a dimension hides the most urgent gaps.
3
Inventory and intake before policyA policy written before the inventory exists is written without knowing what it governs, and is usually rewritten within six months.
