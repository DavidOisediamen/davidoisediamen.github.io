Artefact 14 · Stage 2 · Intake and classification

AI Use-Case Intake Form

The single route by which an AI use case enters governance: what triggers a submission, how its risk tier is set, and what follows from that tier.

v1.1 · 20 November 2026EU AI ActGDPR
Run the triage, 2 minutesRequest the full document
Why it exists

If a use case never enters, no policy applies to it.

Intake is the control on which every other AI governance control depends. A use case that never enters the process is covered by no policy, no assessment and no inventory a regulator could ask for.

The form is designed against one constraint: submission must cost less than avoidance. Sections A to E take under fifteen minutes for a straightforward case, and depth is applied only where the triage says it is warranted.

The rules it enforces

Three rules that make it a control, not a form.

1
Five triggers, any one requires submissionA new use case, a change of purpose, sensitive data, a third-party AI tool (including a feature a vendor switches on) or a change in the applicable standard.
2
The first rule that matches sets the tierRead top down: prohibited practice, then an Annex III domain or three impact flags, then personal or confidential data or an output that decides.
3
Unsure counts as YesAn unresolved answer is treated as the higher-risk answer until the AI Governance Owner resolves it and records why.
What's inside

Eight sections, from requester to business impact.

PartWhat it covers
ARequester and ownership: who submits, and who is accountable for the use case.
BUse case description, including how the output will be used: informs, recommends or decides.
CThe AI system or tool, with vendor disclosure gaps recorded as findings.
DData inputs by category, each with its effect on triage.
EPreliminary risk classification: the prohibited-practice screen, Annex III domains, the impact screen and the provisional tier.
FPrivacy assessment trigger, including inferred personal data.
GHuman oversight design: who reviews, with what authority and information.
HOperational and business impact.

At a glance

  • Stage
    Intake and classification
  • Used by
    Anyone proposing an AI use case; triaged by the AI Governance Owner
  • Time to complete
    Under 15 minutes for sections A to E
  • Triage promised
    Within three working days

Maps to

  • EU AI Act Article 5 (prohibited practices)
  • EU AI Act Annex III (high-risk domains)
  • GDPR Article 22, through the privacy trigger in Section F
  • Artefact 16, for the full applicability test

What changed

  • Two Article 5 prohibitions added, applying from 2 December 2026
  • "Unsure" now counts as Yes
  • Commercially confidential data now lands in Tier 2
