Artefact 15 · Stage 3 · Assessment and risk

AI Risk Register

The operating record of AI risk: taxonomy, scoring model, control effectiveness, residual acceptance authority, and eight worked entries drawn from real failure modes.

v1.1 · 20 November 2026ISO/IEC 42001NIST AI RMF
Full document, attached to the original post
Why it exists

A risk assessment is an event. A register is a living record.

The register is the artefact an auditor asks for first, because it shows whether the assessments produced anything that is being managed. It is the output of the assessment process, not a parallel document.

Its defining feature is that residual risk is accepted by a named individual at an authority set by the score, which keeps escalation objective rather than negotiated.

The rules it enforces

Three rules that make it a control, not a form.

1
Record inherent and residual scoresThe gap between them is the measured value of the control set. A residual alone hides whether the controls are doing any work.
2
The score sets the authorityRisk Owner, AI Governance Owner, Steering Committee Chair or Senior Sponsor, by residual band.
3
Avoid is a legitimate treatmentWhere residual risk cannot be brought below Severe, not proceeding is the process working, not failing.
