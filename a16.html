Artefact 16 · Stage 2 · Intake and classification

EU AI Act Applicability Checklist

A sequential determination, Gate 0 to Gate 8, from "is this even an AI system?" to the conformity route and compliance date, on the post-Omnibus timeline.

v1.1 · 20 November 2026EU AI ActDigital Omnibus
Full document, attached to the original post
Why it exists

Getting the order wrong produces the wrong answer.

Role before tier selects the wrong obligation set. Tier before territorial scope wastes effort on systems that are out of scope entirely.

The checklist enforces the sequence. Each gate carries a documented determination and rationale, producing one defensible position on what applies, to whom and by when.

The rules it enforces

Three rules that make it a control, not a form.

1
Scope firstTerritorial and material scope comes before anything else, including systems used in the Union from outside it.
2
Profiling always defeats the Article 6(3) derogationAnd a system that claims the derogation must still document the assessment and register.
3
A negative determination carries the same burdenConcluding that something does not apply needs the same reasoning and evidence as concluding that it does.
What's inside

Nine gates, then dates and penalties.

PartWhat it covers
0-1Territorial and material scope, and whether it is an AI system.
2-3Prohibited practices under Article 5, and whether it is high-risk by either route.
4-5General-purpose AI model, and role determination with its obligation set.
6Transparency obligations under Article 50.
7-8Technical documentation under Annex IV, and the conformity assessment route.
AppendixCompliance dates and penalty exposure, post-Omnibus.
