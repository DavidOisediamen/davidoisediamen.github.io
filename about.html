How I work

From the obligation to something a team can build and an auditor can test.

1

Start from the obligation

What the regulation or standard actually requires, read at the level of the article or clause, not the headline.

2

Write it as a control

Every control has a trigger, one accountable owner, an exit criterion and evidence that can be produced on request.

3

Translate it for delivery

Requirements, epics, user stories, acceptance criteria and test scripts, so the control gets built and tested rather than filed.

Background

Six years across three regulated sectors.

My governance work spans banking, healthcare and education, each with its own regulatory weight: prudential and conduct rules in banking, patient data protection in healthcare, inspection and data protection in education. The translation from rule to delivery comes from a grounding in business analysis and agile delivery, and the work is growing toward enterprise and business architecture.

The eighteen instruments on this site are the working proof: standards turned into controls, intake and classification, assessment and risk, and the operating model and evidence that keep it running.
