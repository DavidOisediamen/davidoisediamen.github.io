FICTIONAL CASE

One CV screening tool, two regimes.

A recruiter uses one AI tool to rank candidates in Amsterdam and London.

EU: employment is an Annex III domain and ranking candidates profiles them, so the tool is high-risk, with obligations from 2 December 2027 under the Digital Omnibus.

UK: there is no AI statute, but solely automated significant decisions carry four safeguards under UK GDPR Articles 22A to 22D, in force since 5 February 2026.

One control set answers both

  1. A use-case classification form
  2. A user notice and explanation standard
  3. A human-in-the-loop decision gate
  4. A data quality and bias review
  5. An escalation and appeal route
