Privacy notice · Last updated 20 November 2026

This site collects nothing about you.

No cookies, no analytics, no forms, no sign-in. Here is what that means in practice.

Who runs this site

David Oisediamen (Oise OISE). Questions about this notice: davidoise2@outlook.com.

What the site itself collects

Nothing. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and has no forms. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

The intake triage

The triage runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent anywhere or stored, and they are gone when you close the page.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP addresses of visitors to keep its service secure; that processing is GitHub’s, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. I do not receive or use those logs.

If you email me

I use your name, email address and message only to reply, and to send my CV if you ask for it. I do not add you to any list or share your details. I delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed for the conversation.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn, EUR-Lex and other sources take you to sites with their own privacy notices.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, by emailing the address above.
