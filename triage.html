Why this tier

    The four rules, read top down

    • Tier 4Any prohibited practice: do not proceed, escalate at once
    • Tier 3An Annex III domain, special-category data, or three or more impact flags
    • Tier 2Personal data, an output that decides, or one or two impact flags
    • Tier 1None of the above, and no personal or confidential data
